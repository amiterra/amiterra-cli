This policy outlines permitted and prohibited uses of the Amiterra trademarks to prevent public confusion, misleading claims of affiliation, and unauthorized commercial exploitation.

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Written Permission Required)

You may use the Amiterra marks without prior written consent in the following scenarios, provided your use is accurate, truthful, and non-misleading:

1. **Accurate Functional Description:** Referencing the official software, e.g., *"This deployment uses Amiterra CLI to initialize the K3s host"* or *"Compatible with Amiterra v0.1.0"*.
2. **Community Articles, Documentation & Talks:** Writing technical tutorials, blog posts, books, or conference presentations about using Amiterra.
3. **Unaltered Official Binaries:** Distributing official, unmodified binary releases produced by the Amiterra project.
4. **Linking:** Linking to official Amiterra repositories, websites, and release downloads (`https://amiterra.io`, `https://github.com/amiterra/amiterra-cli`).

## 4. Prohibited Uses (Written Permission Required)

You may **not** do any of the following without prior explicit written authorization from Evgenii Myzgin:

1. **Commercial Cloud, Hosting & Managed Services:** You may not use "Amiterra", "ami", or any confusingly similar names in the branding, marketing, or naming of a commercial hosting product, managed database service, cloud tier, VPS/bare-metal package, or SaaS offering (e.g., *"Hetzner Amiterra Service"*, *"Cloud Amiterra Appliance"* are strictly prohibited).
2. **Control Panels, Dashboards & Infrastructure Management Platforms:** You may not use "Amiterra", "ami", or confusingly similar names for any control panel, web dashboard, management console, orchestrator, fleet manager, or control plane designed for managing servers, clusters, workloads, or cloud infrastructure (e.g., *"Amiterra Panel"*, *"Ami Dashboard"*, *"Amiterra Control Center"*, *"Amiterra Manager"* are strictly prohibited).
3. **Company, Product, or Domain Names:** You may not incorporate "Amiterra" or confusingly similar variations into your company name, product name, service name, or top-level domain name (e.g., `amiterra-cloud.com`, `amiterra-panel.io`).
4. **Modified Forks:** If you fork, heavily modify, or recompile the software for distribution outside your internal organization, you **must remove or replace** all Amiterra and `ami` trademarks, logos, and visual identifiers. You may describe your fork as *"Based on the open engine created by Amiterra"*, but not *"Amiterra Custom Edition"*.
5. **False Endorsement:** Using the marks in any manner that falsely implies sponsorship, partnership, certification, or endorsement by the Amiterra team.
